# Fern editorial visual DNA

## Character anchor

- Young female mage with very long deep-purple hair, straight bangs, violet eyes, pale skin, and a calm reserved gaze.
- Oversized matte-black hooded techwear, layered wide trousers or long lower silhouette, silver zippers and chains, restrained violet inner layer.
- Keep the face delicate, body proportions elegant, hands natural, and staff geometry coherent when included.

## Findings from the existing Fern images

- The clean 9:16 source protects a right-side text zone and preserves character identity, but it reads as a three-quarter portrait rather than a true head-to-toe lookbook.
- The V2 cover has strong energy but too many boxes and borders; motion captions compete with the character.
- The best reusable traits are white negative space, diagonal violet geometry, small registration marks, subtle particles, and a left-weighted character.

## Findings from refer1.jpg

- Low viewpoint and complete footwear create authority and fashion-editorial scale.
- Oversized condensed serif letters sit behind the body and establish hierarchy before small copy.
- Warm off-white paper, near-black, and cobalt form a limited print palette with distressed ink texture.
- A monochrome secondary portrait, long vertical copy rail, specimen card, barcode-like marks, and bottom wordmark balance the page.
- Hard directional cast shadow grounds the figure and makes the flat poster feel spatial.

## Adaptation

- Use warm paper white `#F2EEE5`, near-black `#17161C`, cobalt `#184A9C`, and Fern violet `#55327D`.
- Put the main character slightly left or centered, with the face near 30-36% frame height and both feet above the bottom 8% safety line.
- Place the masthead behind hair/body, never over the eyes. Use the right rail for short TTS captions.
- Use one cobalt/violet monochrome secondary silhouette at 12-25% opacity.
- Keep a clear floor plane and cast shadow. Avoid floating feet.
