# Style DNA

## One Line

Modern Korean insta-toon in soft pixel art: chibi proportions, chunky clean dot edges, limited warm palette, small daily-life scene, readable short Korean text.

## Required

- Prefer 1:1 square or 4:5 vertical composition for Instagram.
- Use visible pixel-art qualities: blocky stair-step edges, clustered highlights, simplified shapes, limited palette, crisp silhouette.
- Keep the character cute but modern and calm, not a fantasy character.
- Use modern spaces: desk, room, cafe, subway, office, classroom, street corner, window seat, small studio.
- Use small props that explain the situation: laptop, phone, notebook, checklist, coffee, bag, plant, sticky notes, calendar, delivery box, headset.
- Keep text short: one speech bubble or one overlay per panel when possible.
- Use Korean text only when it carries meaning; avoid decorative text.
- Leave visual breathing room. Do not fill the canvas with tiny props.

## Pixel Look

- Pixelated but readable, like a high-quality indie game dialogue scene or sticker-style pixel illustration.
- Edges should feel built from pixels, not smooth vector curves.
- Use 1 to 3 shadow steps. Avoid airbrushed gradients.
- Use small square highlights on hair, eyes, glasses, or objects.
- Use a coherent palette with warm neutrals plus 1 to 2 accents.

## Text Rules

- Keep speech bubbles short, ideally 8 to 16 Korean characters.
- Keep overlays to 1 or 2 short lines.
- Provide exact text under `Korean text to render exactly`.
- If text becomes unreliable, reduce the wording first before attempting post-processing.

## Bans

- Do not copy a named anime, manga, webtoon, game, or celebrity character.
- Do not copy the reference image composition, character pairing, hair/eye combination, outfit, or plant-on-head motif.
- No elf ears, pointed ears, fantasy robes, capes, armor, magic staffs, spell effects, wings, horns, tails, or fantasy jewelry.
- No heavy painterly rendering, realistic lighting, 3D, glossy vector, or full webtoon splash art.
- No crowded item collection that turns the image into a decorative sticker sheet.
