# Composition Patterns

Choose one pattern per image. Do not mix too many structures.

## Single Moment

Best for one clear emotion or insight.

Draw one character performing one action in a small modern space. Use one speech bubble and one small prop cluster.

## Before / After

Best for transformation, cleanup, or workflow improvement.

Use two compact panels or a split scene. Left side shows confusion; right side shows organized result. Keep the same character but vary expression.

## Choice

Best for tradeoffs or decision points.

Place two cards, doors, tabs, or message boxes in front of the character. The character points, hesitates, or marks one option.

## Hidden Cost

Best for debt, risk, missing context, maintenance, or delayed consequences.

Show the character opening a drawer, box, folder, app tray, or message thread and finding a compact symbol of accumulated work.

## Small Loop

Best for repeated habits, content pipelines, learning cycles, or review loops.

Use 3 small panels: input, action, output. Keep arrows tiny and pixel-styled. Avoid formal flowcharts.

## Conversation Beat

Best for short webtoon scenes.

Use two characters in a modern setting, one line each. Keep character designs distinct through hair, glasses, hat, or clothing, not fantasy traits.

## Scene Density

- One panel: 1 character, 1 to 3 props, 0 to 1 speech bubble.
- Two panels: 1 to 2 characters, 2 to 4 props total, 1 to 2 speech bubbles.
- Three or four panels: tiny panels, one action each, no extra background detail.
